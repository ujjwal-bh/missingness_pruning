# Focused evidence amendment after manuscript feedback

Date 29 October 2026. The researcher approved the focused amendment first. This is a post-review exploratory addition, not a new preregistration. Preserve the original primary ridge targeted-minus-keep-all contrast and both original interval constructions. Do not refit mortality models, select a new budget, or use new target outcomes to choose a method.

1. From the 400 saved paired refitting checkpoints and original fixed-model aggregate bootstrap draws, calculate drop-all minus keep-all for each family/sample. Report AUROC intervals under both constructions; retain probability-metric contrasts where available. Verify differences both directly from checkpoint metrics and by subtracting the two saved targeted contrasts.
2. For all anchor contrasts report original estimate, bootstrap mean/median, bootstrap sample SD (an SE estimate), fraction negative/positive/tied and paired percentile bounds. Sign fractions are empirical draw frequencies, not posterior probabilities or p values. With 200 refitting draws, tail precision and design limitations remain; more draws alone would not remove those limitations.
3. Report targeted performance versus the mean of all 20 fixed random-mask metric values and its descriptive rank, in both families. Do not interpret these 20 controls as a calibrated randomization test or a model-prediction ensemble. No additional masks or outcome-guided budget/ranking search in this amendment.
4. Add an outcome-free selection-only diagnostic with source missingness rates held fixed. Compare 2,000 patient-only adaptation draws, 2,000 hospital-mixture-only draws (whole observed adaptation clusters weighted by hospital draws) and 2,000 hospital/patient draws. Include all 207 hospitals from the existing partition inventory, with zero adaptation contribution from six hospitals. Use identical hospital draws for the latter two schemes. Source sampling, model fitting and target evaluation are held out of this diagnostic. This directly varies adaptation hospital composition; it does not evaluate a new-hospital deployment policy or isolate the cause of the original full-pipeline contrast.
5. Extend the all-24-measurement table with rank, both rates, shift and original/full-pipeline/new selection frequencies. Add calibration-in-the-large and mean risk beside all anchor strategies. Do not attribute external miscalibration mostly to prevalence without a separate decomposition.

Audit event presence, numeric/unit validity, recording availability, screening and consistency with final feature presence. It cannot detect records excluded before import or prove practice differences. Record authentication requirements and leave numerical audit status pending until executed locally; do not fabricate results.
